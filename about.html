About

Chinese traditional colours are a naming system, not just a palette. Each name — moon white, indigo-blue, cinnabar — is a real thing or a real moment, and the colour is a way of remembering it.

Who it is for

Anyone curious about Chinese culture, design, or language — and anyone who wants colours with stories behind them.

How it is built

The site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Every colour lives in one data file, so the wall, the library and the stories all read from the same source.
